## Blockiness campaign

67 rows in [`attempts/v4.1/blockiness.jsonl`](v4.1/blockiness.jsonl).
Harness: [`encoder/v4.1/sweep.py`](../encoder/v4.1/sweep.py). Lab frozen.
Warp-then-SVD was not re-run (t4r killed it, −5.7 to −14 dB).

Two different artifacts:

1. **Moving-element blocks** — butterfly / apple on S06. SVD cannot follow
   local motion inside an 8×8, so the object is a stack of tiles.
2. **8×8 lattice** — sky / grass seams (seamR). The grid is in the model.

**Winner: dense leftover JPEG, stride 1, MAE>3.5, q=40.** That is the
moving-element fix. Stills match the numbers: S06 leftover restores the
butterfly and apple; deblock still looks like SVD; overlap softens the grass
grid but leaves the butterfly faceted.

Stride must be 1. s2/s8 skip the worst frames, so min PSNR stays at the SVD
floor. Tile mosaics are JPEG-header bound at 8×8 (same t4r lesson). Decode
deblock does not replace overlap — the grid is in the model, not a 10% seam
mix (same t3r lesson). Affine / warp-then-SVD is still dead.

Overlap is the lattice path, not the motion path. It is also a denser
representation (15 105 tiles vs 3 840), which is why S06 mean jumps +3.1 dB
at ~4× U. Ship leftover first. Add hop-4 only if the remaining 8×8 on locked
skies is still the demo complaint *and* a ~4× origin is acceptable.

Not shipped into `reconstruct.mp4`. Next product pass on this branch, if
picked: re-pack NAR4 leftover at stride 1 (origin ~21.7 MB, episode 34.82 /
31.34) and leave K′ as-is (leftover still only on the full rung).
